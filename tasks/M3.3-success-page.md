# M3.3: Checkout success page

## Goal
After paying, the student sees "Setting up your access…" until the webhook has granted access, then a confirmation with a "Go to my programmes" button.

## Files you may touch
`src/app/dashboard/billing/success/page.tsx`, `src/app/dashboard/billing/success/refresher.tsx`.

## Steps
1. Server page: read `session_id`, `stripe.checkout.sessions.retrieve(id)`. If `client_reference_id !== current userId`, call `notFound()` (a student must never see someone else's session).
2. Look up the user's entitlements in the DB (plain query for now; `lib/access.ts` arrives in M4.3).
   - Entitlements exist for the purchased product → a **highlight card** (per DESIGN.md) with a check icon, "Bārak Allāhu fīk, your access is ready" + "Go to my programmes".
   - Not yet → "Setting up your access…" + `<Refresher />`.
3. `refresher.tsx` (client): `router.refresh()` every 2 s, stopping after 30 s. Then show "This is taking longer than usual. Your payment is safe; refresh in a minute or contact support."
