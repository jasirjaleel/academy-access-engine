# M4.4: Student dashboard home (premium look)

## Goal
The first screen AMAR sees in the demo. Calm, premium and readable on a phone. Islamic-studies tone: no gamified confetti, just clean typography and generous spacing.

## Files you may touch
`src/app/dashboard/page.tsx`, `src/app/dashboard/layout.tsx`, `src/components/dashboard/*`, `src/lib/dashboard.ts`, `src/app/layout.tsx` (font only), `src/app/globals.css` (theme tokens only).

## Steps
1. Fonts and tokens are already set up in M2.4 (Inter + Noto Naskh Arabic). Arabic text always renders in `<span dir="rtl" lang="ar" className="font-arabic">`.
2. **Follow `docs/DESIGN.md` exactly** (dark midnight navy, amber accents, ring-1 white/10 cards). Use its recipes: amber progress bars, badge styles, the highlight card for the free programme, the warning banner for payment problems, and the amber play button on "continue watching".
3. `src/lib/dashboard.ts`, `getDashboardData(db, userId)`, in one place: programme access (`getProgrammeAccess`), progress per programme (completed / total lessons), the latest incomplete lesson ("continue watching"), and billing summary (active subscriptions with `current_period_end`, lifetime yes/no, any entitlement in `grace` with its `grace_until`).
4. `dashboard/layout.tsx`: the site header + a container (max-w-5xl).
5. Dashboard page sections:
   - **Greeting:** "Assalāmu ʿalaykum, {firstName}" + a one-line subtitle.
   - **Payment problem banner** (only when something is in grace): amber, "Your last payment didn't go through. Your access continues until {date}. Update your card →" (the link goes to `/dashboard/billing`; M5 wires the portal).
   - **Continue watching** card (if any): lesson title EN/AR, programme, a progress bar, "Resume" button.
   - **My programmes:** 3 cards (EN title + Arabic title, description, `Progress` bar with "4 of 6 lessons", a badge `Active` / `Included free` / `Locked`). Unlocked → "Open"; locked → "Unlock" (→ `/pricing`).
   - **Billing card:** plan name(s), "Renews on {date}" or "Lifetime access", link "Manage billing" (→ `/dashboard/billing`).
6. Loading: `loading.tsx` with shadcn Skeletons in the same layout.

## ✅ Check (Jasir runs)
- At 375 px width (DevTools mobile) nothing overflows, and the Arabic titles render right-to-left in Naskh.
- A user with no purchases: all 3 cards show `Locked` (usool also needs paid access, since it's a bundle), and each programme's preview lesson is still reachable.
- After buying Arabic: Arabic `Active`, usool `Included free`, Precision `Locked`.
- Manually set an entitlement to `grace` with `grace_until` in the future (Supabase table editor) → the amber banner appears.
