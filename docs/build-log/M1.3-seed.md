# M1.3: Seed programmes and lessons

## Goal
An **idempotent** seed script that creates the 3 programmes and their lessons. Running it twice must not create duplicates.

## Files you may touch
`src/db/seed.ts` only.

## Steps
1. The script loads `.env.local` (dotenv) and creates its own postgres client (don't import `src/db/index.ts`, because of the `server-only` import).
2. Upsert programmes on `slug` (`onConflictDoUpdate`). Upsert lessons on `(programme_id, position)`.
3. Use **exactly** this content. Do not invent other Arabic text:

**arabic**: "Arabic Programme" / "البرنامج العربي", position 1
| pos | title_en | title_ar | min |

| 1 | The Arabic alphabet | الحروف الهجائية | 12 |
| 2 | Short vowels | الحركات القصيرة | 14 |
| 3 | Sukoon and shaddah | السكون والشدة | 11 |
| 4 | Tanween | التنوين | 10 |
| 5 | Connecting and separating letters | وصل الحروف وفصلها | 15 |
| 6 | The sun and moon letters | اللام الشمسية واللام القمرية | 13 |

**precision**: "Precision in Knowledge" / "الدقة في العلم", position 2
